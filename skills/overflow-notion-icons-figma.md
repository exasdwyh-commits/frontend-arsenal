## The cleanest Figma-to-Notion icon workflow
The right way to use these in Figma is to keep the vectors editable until the end. That lets you compare styles, keep the interface consistent, and export only the exact files you need.

  - ![An editable Figma frame with vector artwork]

### Start with the editable Figma file
Use Figma as the source of truth while you test icon choices in dashboards, docs, templates or UI mockups.
- ![Regular and duotone icon styles compared in one layout]

### Compare styles before export
Try regular and duotone inside the actual layout first, so you do not lock the wrong visual tone too early.
- ![Exporting a square PNG for a Notion upload]

### Export PNG for direct uploads
When the icon is going straight into Notion, export a square transparent PNG. That is the safest upload format for the product itself.
- ![An SVG source branching to docs and developer handoff]

### Keep SVG for reuse and handoff
SVG is better as a reusable source for design systems, docs, or developers who need clean vector assets outside the Notion upload flow.

## Why Figma is the best place to choose Notion icons first
It is easier to make the right icon decision in context. Figma lets you see how an icon behaves beside text, cards, templates and product UI before the asset gets uploaded into Notion.

## FAQ
Working in Figma, and what to export from it.

**Can I use Overflow Notion icons in Figma?**
: Yes. The Notion Icons library includes Figma source files, so you can work with the icons directly inside Figma before exporting PNG or SVG.

**What should I export from Figma for Notion uploads?**
: Keep the editable master in Figma, then export a transparent square PNG at about 280x280 px for the actual Notion upload. SVG is better as a source or developer handoff format.

**Are there free Notion icons for Figma?**
: Yes. 194 icons are free, and the library is available as a Figma Community file alongside PNG and SVG exports.

**Is there a Figma plugin for these icons?**
: Yes. Overflow Icons & Illustrations, free on Figma Community, searches every icon, illustration and isometric drawing from a panel in Figma or FigJam and inserts it with a click or a drag. The 194 free icons insert for anyone; a Pro key opens the rest.

**Which Notion icon style works best in Figma?**
: That depends on the interface tone you want. Regular icons are the cleanest baseline and duotone adds more depth without leaving the Notion aesthetic.
