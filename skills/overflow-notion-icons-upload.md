## The fastest way to upload a custom icon
Notion keeps icon controls close to the page title, so the process is simple once your file is ready. The main thing is uploading the right asset, not hunting for the menu.

  - ![Clicking the page icon above a Notion page title]

### 1. Open the icon menu
Open the Notion page where you want the icon and click the icon placeholder or current icon above the page title.
- ![Choosing the upload option in the Notion icon menu]

### 2. Choose upload
Use the upload option so you can bring in your own custom file rather than relying only on emojis or Notion defaults.
- ![A square transparent PNG file prepared for Notion]

### 3. Upload a square PNG
A transparent square PNG is the cleanest direct-upload format. Around 280x280 px works well, which is the size every icon on this site downloads at.
- ![Replacing or changing a Notion page icon later]

### 4. Replace it later if needed
Click the same icon area again whenever you want to swap the design, test another style, or remove the icon entirely.

## What usually makes Notion icons look off
Most icon problems come from the export, not from Notion itself. If the file is cramped, oversized, or coming from the wrong source format, the upload usually looks weaker than it should.

  - ![Icon artwork touching the square export edges]

### No padding around the artwork
If the drawing touches the square edges, it tends to look cramped in Notion. Leave breathing room around the shape before exporting.
- ![Keeping SVG as the source and uploading PNG to Notion]

### Uploading the wrong format first
Keep SVG or Figma as the editable source, but upload PNG into Notion when you want the simplest and safest result.
- ![A slide deck export compared with a Notion-ready icon]

### Reusing a file made for another surface
A file exported for docs, decks or product UI is not always the best upload asset for Notion. Prepare a square PNG specifically for this slot.
- ![Picking an icon style after many pages already use icons]

### Choosing the style too late
It is easier to stay visually consistent if you settle on regular or duotone before you start uploading icons across multiple pages.

## FAQ
Uploading, formats and why an icon can look cramped.

**How do I add a custom icon to Notion?**
: Open the page icon above the Notion page title, choose the upload option and select your PNG file. After that, you can replace or remove the icon from the same menu.

**Should I upload PNG or SVG to Notion?**
: For direct uploads into Notion, PNG is the safest choice. Keep SVG or Figma as the source format for editing and export a transparent PNG when you are ready to upload.

**Why does my Notion icon look too small or cramped?**
: Usually the artwork is either touching the square edges or exported at an awkward size. Leave padding around the icon and use a square PNG prepared for Notion, around 280x280 px.
