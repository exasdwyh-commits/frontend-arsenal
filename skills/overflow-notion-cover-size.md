## How to keep Notion covers and header images looking clean
Notion changes the visible crop depending on device size, page layout and browser width. The easiest way to keep a cover safe is to design wide, keep the centre clean, and avoid putting delicate details near the edges.

  - ![A wide landscape Notion cover compared with a tall image being cropped harshly]

### Design wide, not tall
A landscape banner works best because Notion uses a wide cover slot. Tall artwork usually gets cropped too aggressively to feel intentional.
- ![A Notion cover with a safe centre band and softer edge zones]

### Treat the edges as disposable
Keep the most important part of the visual in the centre band. Edges can be trimmed differently on desktop and mobile, so they should carry less critical detail.
- ![Bold shapes in the centre, with tiny detail at the edges avoided]

### Avoid tiny text in the artwork
Covers are usually seen as atmospheric headers, not readable posters. Use shape, contrast and broad composition instead of fine print.
- ![A Figma frame exporting to a single PNG file]

### Export one clean PNG
Once the crop feels right in Figma, export a single PNG for upload. Keep the editable source separately so future cover changes are quick.

## FAQ
Dimensions, aspect ratio and where the crop falls.

**What is the best Notion cover size?**
: A practical default is a wide image around 1500x600 px. That gives you enough room for Notion's desktop crop while still staying lightweight.

**What aspect ratio works best for Notion covers?**
: A wide banner-style image works best. Notion crops covers differently across desktop and mobile, so a landscape composition with a safe centre usually performs better than a tall or tightly framed image.

**Should I keep text away from the edges of a Notion cover?**
: Yes. Keep important elements close to the centre because Notion can crop the edges differently across screen sizes.
