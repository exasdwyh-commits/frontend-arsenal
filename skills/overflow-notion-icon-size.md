## How to keep icons crisp inside Notion
Notion scales icons down hard in the interface, so clean geometry matters more than oversized files. Export square files, preserve transparency, and avoid cramming artwork to the very edge.

  - ![A PNG file ready for transparent upload to Notion]

### Use PNG for uploads
When you upload a custom icon into Notion, a transparent PNG is the easiest file to manage. It behaves predictably and avoids format surprises.
- ![Padding between icon artwork and the square export edge]

### Keep padding around the artwork
Do not let strokes or fills touch the edges of the square. A little breathing room usually makes the icon feel sharper at small sizes.
- ![Figma and SVG as editable source files]

### Keep SVG or Figma as the master
Build and edit the icon in vector form first, then export the PNG size you need for Notion. That gives you one clean source for future changes.
- ![A 280px Notion upload compared with a 512px export for reuse]

### Go larger only when reuse matters
If the icon also needs to appear in docs, decks, mockups or dev handoff, export a 512x512 PNG alongside the smaller upload file.

## FAQ
Sizes, formats and the padding question.

**What is the best Notion icon size?**
: For direct Notion uploads, a square transparent PNG around 280x280 px is a practical default. If you want extra export headroom, 512x512 px is a safe larger option.

**Should I use PNG or SVG for Notion icons?**
: Use PNG for uploading a custom icon into Notion itself, and keep SVG or Figma as the master source for editing, scaling and developer handoff.

**Do Notion icons need padding?**
: Yes. Leave breathing room around the icon so it does not touch the square export edges. That usually makes the icon look cleaner in Notion's compact UI.
